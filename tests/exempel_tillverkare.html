Wax tips for Vasaloppet

Expected -6°C at the start, old fine grained snow, icy tracks in the first climbs.

Kick

  • Binder: Swix KB20 Green Base Klister, ironed in thin
  • Swix KX30 covered with V40, 3 layers

Glide

Swix PS7 as base layer, then TS7 on top.

Warm alternative

If it gets warmer than -2: KX45 with a thin cover of V45.
